## What's new

- 13 more fonts, each with its own character: Atkinson Hyperlegible (made for low vision), Barlow
  Semi Condensed, Fredoka, Comic Neue, Righteous, MedievalSharp, Uncial Antiqua, Orbitron, Russo One,
  Black Ops One, JetBrains Mono, Pixelify Sans and Special Elite. Inter SemiBold stays the default
- New settings page: a table of every font, each drawn in its own font, with a large preview, the
  letters each font covers (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek) and which addon it comes from
- The settings page shows when a reload is needed and has a Reload UI button, instead of a popup after
  every change
